Compound dictionary

This notebook uses selected Japanese and English entries from JMdict, © James William Breen and the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group, under CC BY-SA 4.0. See the EDRDG licence statement and dictionary documentation.

We select up to 20 common compound forms per kanji, preserve paired readings and applicable English senses, and prioritize words introduced in the notebook. This is a broader study selection, not a complete dictionary or an N5 word list. Irregular readings such as 火傷 (やけど) are retained. The derived dictionary data remains under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Download the attributed compound dataset. To update it, run npm run update:compounds from the project folder, then rebuild and restart the app. Refresh at least monthly when hosting or distributing dictionary data.
